# Product: Phantom Inbox

## Pitch

Every scam has a ghost hiding in it. Phantom Inbox makes it visible.

Phantom Inbox is a scam detector for emails, SMS and WhatsApp messages. It focuses on scams that are common in India.

## Target users

- Non-technical adults who get suspicious messages and want a quick, clear answer.
- Elderly parents, who need large text, high contrast and plain words.
- Scam-awareness learners, who want to practise spotting scams.

## Scam categories

- KYC expiry (a message says your bank or wallet account will be blocked unless you update KYC, which means "know your customer" identity details).
- Courier and customs fees (a parcel is "held" until you pay a small fee).
- Electricity bill disconnection (power will be cut tonight unless you pay or call).
- UPI refunds and collect requests (UPI is India's instant payment system; a "refund" that makes you approve a payment request).
- Fake job and task offers (easy money for liking videos or doing small tasks).
- "Digital arrest" (a caller or message claims you are under arrest and must pay or stay on a video call).

## Features

There are exactly three features.

- **Possession Check:** analyses a pasted message and shows which parts look like a scam and why.
- **Exorcism:** tells the user what to do next and gives a short warning to share with family.
- **Séance:** a training game where the user decides whether a message is real or a scam.

## Views

- **Mortal view:** calm, high contrast and large text, built for elders. It uses plain wording and no decoration that gets in the way.
- **Haunted view:** a dark haunted mansion where each red flag appears as a ghost on the exact words it is about. The ghost always comes with a text label and a one-line reason.

The spooky design exists to make the reasons behind each red flag easier to understand. It must never make them harder to read or follow.

## Honesty about certainty

- Phantom Inbox does not claim 100% certainty. No result is a guarantee that a message is safe or a scam.
- Every result is a risk level with at least 1 reason the user can read and check.

## Non-goals

- Backend services: everything runs in the browser.
- Paid APIs: no paid or keyed services.
- User accounts: no sign-in.
- Message storage: no message is saved or sent anywhere.
- Tracking: no analytics, no third-party scripts.
